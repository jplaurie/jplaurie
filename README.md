# Jason Laurie

**Applied mathematics · Fluid dynamics · Wave turbulence · Nonlinear optics · Scientific computing**

I am an applied mathematician and scientific programmer with a background in
fluid dynamics, statistical physics, nonlinear waves, and turbulence. My work
uses mathematical modelling and numerical simulation to study classical and
quantum fluids, vortex dynamics, and nonlinear optical systems.

I previously worked in academia as a Senior Lecturer in Applied Mathematics at
Aston University. I maintain the public repositories below as open research
software, learning resources, and a record of my computational work.

## Public projects

| Project | Description | Format / technologies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [2dPointVortex](https://github.com/jplaurie/2dPointVortex) | Two-dimensional point-vortex dynamics in the infinite plane, a periodic box, and a circular disk | C++20, OpenMP, MPI, CUDA |
| [2dNavierStokes](https://github.com/jplaurie/2dNavierStokes) | Dealiased pseudo-spectral solver for two-dimensional Navier–Stokes turbulence | C++20, FFTW, OpenMP, MPI, CUDA |
| [2dGrossPitaevskii](https://github.com/jplaurie/2dGrossPitaevskii) | Pseudo-spectral Gross–Pitaevskii/nonlinear Schrödinger solver for Bose–Einstein condensates and nonlinear optics | C++20, FFTW, OpenMP, MPI, CUDA |
| [1dSchrodingerHelmholtz](https://github.com/jplaurie/1dSchrodingerHelmholtz) | Dealiased pseudo-spectral solver for the one-dimensional Schrödinger–Helmholtz equation, its long-wave limit, and the cubic nonlinear Schrödinger equation | C++20, FFTW, OpenMP |
| [GermanBook](https://github.com/jplaurie/GermanBook) | An evolving German grammar and verb-reference book developed as part of my German-learning journey | LaTeX |

The research-software projects stem from numerical codes developed during my academic research. The multidimensional projects include backends for a single CPU,
multiple CPUs, or an NVIDIA GPU with CUDA. The one-dimensional projects remain bound to CPU usage only due to ineffectiveness of parallalisation. Each project also includes analysis and plotting tools.

## Research interests

- Fluid dynamics and turbulence, including two-dimensional and geophysical flows
- Quantum turbulence, Bose–Einstein condensates, and superfluid helium
- Wave turbulence, nonlinear optics, and coherent structures
- Vortex dynamics, numerical methods, and high-performance scientific computing
